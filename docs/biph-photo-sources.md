## Verification

The official `biph.basischina.com` application maps school code `BIPH` to BASIS International School Park Lane Harbour / 华润小径湾贝赛思国际学校. Its Our School page loads the BIPH campus carousel from [the official home data bundle](https://biph.basischina.com/js/pages-home-datas-home.3fcb55ce.js); the bundle explicitly labels these two images “Overview” and “Main Gate” under the BIPH key. The official library page loads [its library data bundle](https://biph.basischina.com/js/pages-student_life-data-library.9b7b669e.js), which assigns the library image below to BIPH. The site’s public content API also describes this school’s campus in Park Lane Harbour, Guangdong.

## Composition guidance

- `biph-campus-overview.jpg` is the strongest directory header: it shows the actual campus architecture clearly with no identifiable people. Its 2.4:1 ratio closely matches a 440 × 176 header.
- `biph-main-gate.jpg` is the strongest alternate/profile header. The school name is visible on the building. People are present along the lower edge; they are part of the original official campus photo.
- `biph-library.jpg` has no people but is only 288 × 180. Keep it small; do not stretch it for a large header.
